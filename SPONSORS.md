EEF is free to use — all 38 skills, the install script, and the plugin
manifest stay open. Sponsoring funds the time to keep porting stacks,
closing gaps, and fixing what breaks.

Sponsor via [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/edhoferdian).

  written and tested, not on the public schedule.

Everything in Backer, plus:

  using a specific skill in your own project.

## One-time sponsorship

Any one-time amount is welcome and listed the same as a Supporter-tier
recurring sponsor, for as long as the README's Sponsors section is
maintained.

## What sponsorship does not buy

No SLA on response time, no custom skill development, no private support
channel outside GitHub. That's what an Enterprise arrangement is for —
if that's what you need, open an issue titled "Enterprise inquiry" or
reach out at edhoferdian31@gmail.com and we'll figure out what fits.
