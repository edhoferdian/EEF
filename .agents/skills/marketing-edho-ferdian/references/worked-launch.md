# Worked Launch — positioning to landing copy to a launch week

One small product taken through `positioning-interview.md`,
`brand-voice-framework.md`, `copywriting-patterns.md` and
`platform-content.md`. Copy is in Indonesian (the audience's language);
notes are in English. Facts about the product come from the user — never
invent numbers, testimonials or customers.

## The product (user-supplied)

Kasirku: a phone-first POS for small coffee shops. Records sales, QRIS
marked by the cashier, daily report. Rp49.000/month, 30-day free trial.
Built by one developer. Current users: 3 shops (friends).

## 1. Positioning (one paragraph, then the parts)

> For owners of small coffee shops who run the counter themselves,
> Kasirku is a cashier app on the phone they already have that shows
> today's sales in one screen. Unlike full POS systems that need a tablet,
> a printer and setup, Kasirku starts in five minutes.

| Part | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Owner-operators of 1-outlet coffee shops | Narrow beats "UMKM" — they feel the pain daily |
| Category | "Aplikasi kasir di HP" | Words they already search |
| Alternative they use now | Notebook + calculator, or a heavy POS | Defines the comparison |
| Key benefit | Know today's sales without counting | Their stated pain |
| Proof | 3 shops using it (with permission to name?) | Ask the user — don't invent |

## 2. Voice

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Plain, warm, practical ("kamu") | Corporate ("solusi terintegrasi") |
| Specific (numbers, steps) | Hype ("revolusioner", "terbaik") |
| Short sentences | Jargon (POS, SaaS, dashboard analytics) |

## 3. Landing page copy

```markdown
# Kasir di HP untuk kedai kopi kamu
Catat penjualan, terima QRIS, dan lihat omzet hari ini — tanpa tablet, tanpa printer.
[Coba gratis 30 hari]  ·  Tanpa kartu kredit

## Kenapa Kasirku
**Omzet hari ini, satu layar.** Tutup kedai tanpa hitung ulang nota.
**Siap dalam 5 menit.** Masukkan menu, langsung jualan.
**Cash dan QRIS tercatat rapi.** Laporan harian terpisah per metode bayar.

## Harga
Rp49.000/bulan · semua fitur · gratis 30 hari pertama · berhenti kapan saja

## Pertanyaan
**Perlu internet?** Ya, koneksi HP biasa cukup.
**Datanya aman?** Tersimpan di server dengan cadangan harian; hanya kamu yang bisa melihat.
**Bisa lebih dari satu kasir?** Belum. Kasirku dibuat untuk kedai dengan satu kasir.

[Coba gratis 30 hari]
```

Patterns used: headline = audience + outcome; sub-head = how + the
objection removed; benefits as outcome-first bold lines; one CTA repeated;
honest limits in the FAQ (they build trust and filter the wrong users).

## 4. Launch week plan

| Day | Channel | Piece | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Instagram (Reels) | 20-s screen recording: one sale → the daily report | Show, don't tell |
| Tue | WhatsApp groups of coffee-shop owners (where the user is a member) | Short personal message + link | Trial sign-ups |
| Wed | Instagram carousel | "3 tanda kamu butuh aplikasi kasir" | Saves/shares |
| Thu | Personal outreach | DM 20 owners the user knows | Conversations |
| Fri | Instagram story | Behind the scenes: "dibuat sendiri, ini alasannya" | Trust |
| Sat | Follow-up | Message trial users who haven't recorded a sale | Activation |

Instagram Reel script (20 s):

```text
0–3 s   Text: "Tutup kedai, masih hitung nota?" (face to camera or hands with notebook)
3–12 s  Screen: add 2 kopi susu → QRIS → sale saved (real app, no mockup)
12–17 s Screen: daily report — total, cash vs QRIS
17–20 s Text + voice: "Kasirku. Coba gratis 30 hari — link di bio."
Caption: Kasir di HP buat kedai kopi. Catat jualan, lihat omzet hari ini. Gratis 30 hari 👉 link di bio
```

## 5. Measure

| Metric | Where | Week-1 target (set with the user) |
|---|---|---|
| Landing visits | analytics | — |
| Trial sign-ups | app DB | e.g. 15 |
| Activated (≥ 1 sale in 3 days) | app DB | e.g. 50 % of sign-ups |
| Reply rate on outreach | manual | — |

Review on day 7: which channel produced activated users (not just
sign-ups), and double down on that one.

## Rules this example follows

- Every claim traceable to the user's facts (price, trial, features, 3 shops).
- No fake testimonials or "dipercaya 1.000+ UMKM".
- The copy is passed through `humanize-edho-ferdian` (when installed) to
  sound like Ed before publishing.
- Publishing and sending messages need the user's go-ahead.
